# Logo sources

Maker logos for the benchmark charts (`scripts/logos.py`). They are trademarks of their owners and are used
only to show who made each model.

- `openai.png`, `nvidia.png`, `ibm.png`, `mistral.png`, `qwen.png`, `alibaba.png`, `huggingface.png`:
  rasterised from the SVGs in LobeHub's icon set, `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.101.0 (MIT licence).
- `astar.png`: the A*STAR "a*" mark, cropped from the share image on a-star.edu.sg.
- `whisperlah.png`: drawn for this repo.

Distil-Whisper shows the Hugging Face logo, SenseVoice the Alibaba logo, and the MERaLiON models the A*STAR mark.
